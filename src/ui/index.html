Custom Integrated Circuits in ProLogic
A custom integrated circuit allows you to package any combination of logic gates, flip-flops, and subcircuits into a compact, reusable modular chip with custom pins.
1
Configure Chip Input Pins
Place Toggle Switches on your circuit and assign their "Export Pin Name" in the Inspector (these become chip inputs).
2
Configure Chip Output Pins
Place Light Bulbs on your circuit and assign their "Export Pin Name" in the Inspector (these become chip outputs).
3
Package Into a Reusable IC
Click "Package IC" in the toolbar to turn your design into a reusable chip ready for schematic placement!
